Use case: logo-brand.
Create a beautifully refined professional logo presentation for BScout, a Russian search and price comparison service for electronics parts, with ambition to become a major technology platform. This is a NEW original identity, not an imitation of another company.
Mandatory subject: an instantly legible capital Latin B with TWO rabbit ears seamlessly integrated into the TOP of the B. The B must still clearly have a vertical stem and two enclosed counters / bowls, with the rabbit ears projecting above. Design a single compact, bold, memorable silhouette. Ears are purposeful and slightly asymmetric, one subtly angled as if alert/listening. Friendly intelligence, precision, speed, confidence. Not a rabbit face or full rabbit. No eyes, whiskers or cartoon details. The negative spaces in B should be clean and generous for small size. Beautiful optical balancing.
Typography: custom substantial contemporary rounded grotesk wordmark spelled EXACTLY "BScout" (capital B, capital S, lowercase cout). Refined bespoke letter shapes, approachable technology, slightly tight professional kerning, no italic, no thin strokes, no generic futuristic font. Icon to left of wordmark with enough breathing room.
Palette: vivid warm signal orange #FF5A1F for the primary symbol; near-black charcoal #191919 wordmark; warm off-white #F7F5F0 canvas. Flat solid colors only. Strong enough to work in one color.
Composition: one art-directed brand identity sheet in landscape 3:2. Upper approximately two-thirds: large primary horizontal lockup, symbol plus BScout, centered with abundant negative space. Subtle fine horizontal separator. Lower third: three smaller evenly balanced demonstrations of THE EXACT SAME symbol geometry: left, black monochrome horizontal lockup on warm white; middle, white reverse horizontal lockup on a simple charcoal rectangular area; right, orange rounded-square app tile with white rabbit-ear B. This tile is a tiny application, not a giant mockup. Keep every mark geometrically consistent. Very small restrained label "BScout / Identity concept" at top left is optional. No other text or slogan.
Style: exceptional vector-like graphic design, crisp smooth edges, flat orthographic clean logo artwork, premium independent brand studio presentation. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no paper texture, no photography, no device mockups, no stock bunny icon, no checkmark, no magnifying glass, no arrows, no imitation of Yandex or other brands. Deliver one confident resolved design, not a collection of different proposals.
